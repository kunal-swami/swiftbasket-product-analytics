# Prioritized Product Recommendations

Scores use a five-point Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort model.

| Rank | Insight | Proposed change | Impact | Confidence | Effort | Score | Metric | Risk |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Sub-₹399 carts show the sharpest payment-attempt decline | Test a first-order handling-fee waiver below ₹399 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 6.7 | Seven-day conversion | Margin dilution and discount dependence |
| 2 | Transparency experiment was inconclusive | Run a powered transparency-only test and measure support/trust outcomes | 3 | 4 | 2 | 6.0 | Cart-to-order and fee-related contacts | Earlier abandonment without order lift |
| 3 | Meta Ads share increased while conversion remained lowest | Tighten targeting and optimize to converted users, not installs | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5.3 | Converted users/CAC | Reduced acquisition volume |
| 4 | Aggregate conversion hides cart-band friction | Add a weekly new-user checkout health monitor by cart band/channel | 3 | 5 | 2 | 7.5 | Checkout-to-payment rate | Alert fatigue if thresholds are noisy |

Recommendation four has the highest mathematical score but is an enabling analytics action, not the direct product fix. Recommendation one remains the first product experiment.
